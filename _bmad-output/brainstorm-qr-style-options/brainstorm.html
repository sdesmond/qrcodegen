2 Technique / One Feature Only

If you could only build one: the dots

userDots are the bulk of the QR code, so they win the spotlight. The rest of the layers dim.

  • coachNeighbor-aware rendering: round the outer corners only where no neighbor exists (connected blobs via SVG path union).
  • coachDot size / roundness slider with a live grade. tunable
  • coachGradient dots; clamp stops so contrast stays decodable.
  • userPremium dots = gradients + colors, spacing and corner roundness as parametric controls. sliders
  • userSecond feature: the center logo, the one real way to personalize.
  • coachShape-aware clear zone and a recomputed EC budget instead of a fixed 10-30%.
  • userDots near the logo fade out with a soft falloff.
  • coachFade by shrinking dot size, not opacity: decoders binarize, gray mush hurts.
  • insightParametric dots subsume most discrete shapes: the gallery becomes presets.
