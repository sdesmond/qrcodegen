# Accessibility Review — QR Code Generator

## Verdict
The spines state the right intentions (radio groups, aria-labels, ring as shape cue, theme-specific danger tokens), but several load-bearing decisions fail or are underspecified for WCAG 2.2 AA: the selection ring and the disabled/locked states fall below 3:1 in places, the stale state has no non-visual or low-vision-robust path beyond one live-region sentence, and the keyboard and focus model (roving tabindex, focus after Fix, floating Generate vs focus-not-obscured, scrollable strips) is mostly implied rather than specified. Not ready to build from without a revision pass; most fixes are small spec additions, not redesigns.

Notes on method: ratios were computed from the hex tokens (WCAG relative luminance); translucent fills were alpha-blended over the parent surface. The mocks are static spans/divs (only the preset thumbnails are `button role=radio`; Fix, Generate, Download are `div`/`span`), so semantics were judged from the spine text, not the mocks. The mocks have no `:focus-visible` rules, and the baseline page sets `outline:none` on inputs and range sliders and relies on a 15%-alpha accent box-shadow, which the new components must not inherit.

## Findings

### Contrast and non-text contrast
- **[high]** The selected-state ring (`accent` #6c63ff, 2px, 2px offset) is below 3:1 against several backgrounds it sits on: 3.46:1 on dark surface-2 passes, but 3.90 on dark surface is marginal, and on light the ring against surface-2-light is 4.03 and against white 4.32. It passes in both themes where the offset gap shows the surface color, so the ring itself is OK. The real failure is the *unselected* thumbnail/button boundary, `border` on `surface`/`input`: 1.35 to 1.47 dark and 1.23 to 1.30 light. Preset thumbnails, direction buttons, saved-logo tiles, color fields and the dashed drop zone are identified only by a 1.2-1.5:1 border (DESIGN.md Components, 1.4.11). The thumbnail artwork itself (dark code on light tile) may supply the 3:1 boundary for presets, but direction buttons (icon only, `muted` glyph on surface-2: 4.82 dark, 4.52 light, passes) and the empty/low-contrast logo tiles do not have a guaranteed one. *Fix:* require a UI-component boundary of at least 3:1 (e.g. `text-muted` border, or a darker/lighter border token) on all new interactive controls, or document that the artwork is the identifying boundary and enforce a minimum fill contrast for logo tiles.
- **[high]** Disabled/locked treatments are stated as opacity values (Generate 0.5, Download 0.4, EC locked 0.35). Disabled controls are exempt from 1.4.3, but the *reason hint* is not, and EC locked at 0.35 renders the locked "H" selection at about 1.9:1, so a sighted low-vision user cannot tell which level is locked in. Computed: disabled Generate label about 2.7:1 (dark), 2.0:1 (light); Download-off label 3.5:1; locked EC label 1.9:1 (DESIGN.md Components "Locked control note", "Generate / Download"). *Fix:* keep the locked-to-H value at full or at least 3:1 contrast (dim only the other three), and put the lock icon plus text in the hint, not in the dimming alone.
- **[medium]** `accent-2` teal is used as text for the "Generated" banner, content pills and hover, and it is shared across themes: 8.81:1 on dark surface but about 1.9:1 on white (light theme). The "Generated" banner text and any selected-pill text fail 1.4.3 in light theme. DESIGN.md only addresses danger. *Fix:* add `accent-2-light` (a darker teal, about #007a63 gives roughly 5:1 on white) for text and border use; also covers the pill baseline.
- **[medium]** White on `accent` #6c63ff is 4.32:1 (inherited, flagged ASSUMPTION). The Generate gradient end #8b5cf6 with white is 4.23:1. Active tab, Solid/Gradient active option and Generate label at 0.78 to 0.95rem are normal-size text, so 4.32/4.23 fail AA 4.5. The spec marks it "inherited"; but the new Solid/Gradient toggle and floating Generate add *new* uses. *Fix:* darken the accent for filled-with-white-text uses only (about #5b52f0 gives roughly 5.4:1) or make toggle label bold 14px+ is not enough (needs 18.66px bold for large). Decide it now rather than leaving an assumption.
- **[medium]** Accent used as text (EC selected, slider value, "slider span") is 3.90:1 on dark surface and 4.32:1 on white: fails 4.5:1 for 0.8rem text. New components that reuse this pattern (selected toggle labels on tinted fills) inherit it.
- **[medium]** Light-theme muted text `#6b7280` is 4.32:1 on `bg-light` #f0f2f8 (fails), 4.52 on surface-2-light, 4.6 on input-light, 4.83 on white. Section titles and hints (0.65rem, 0.72rem) sit on `bg`/`surface` in places; the 0.65rem uppercase section title on bg-light fails. DESIGN.md claims the light neutral swap but never states muted ratios. *Fix:* darken `text-muted-light` to about #5b6270 (roughly 5.5:1 on #f0f2f8).
- **[medium]** Danger text on its own 12% tint: 4.84:1 dark, 4.71:1 light. Passes, but with little margin; the doc says text is "about 5.6:1 on surface", which is the wrong background for the banner (the banner background is the tint). Document the actual pair. Fix button: dark text #0f1117 on #ff5c7c = 6.36:1 pass; white on #c4234a = 5.70:1 pass; white on #ff5c7c = 2.97 correctly rejected.
- **[medium]** The stale code at 0.4 opacity: black modules on white fade to #999 on white = 2.85:1; a user gradient (e.g. #6c63ff modules) fades to roughly 1.7:1. The old code is visible but near-invisible for low vision, and brand gradients make it worse (1.4.11 does not apply to a disabled image, but the information "this is the old code" is lost for exactly the users who cannot perceive the dimming change reliably). See state section for the broader issue.
- **[low]** Focus indicator: the spec names "focus ring" as accent use but gives no spec. The baseline `input:focus` replaces the outline with a border-color change plus a 15%-alpha 3px shadow, which the 15% glow reuse (changed-by-fix) shares. The glow at 15% accent on dark surface is far below 3:1. Border changes alone from #2e3347 to #6c63ff are 3.90:1 against surface, which would pass 2.4.13 only on inputs; buttons and radios in the mocks have none. *Fix:* DESIGN.md must define one global `:focus-visible` (2px solid, 3:1 against both adjacent colors, with a 2px offset; e.g. `accent` ring plus a contrasting inner halo) used by every new control, including thumbnails inside a scroll container (overflow clipping can hide an offset ring; use inset or add strip padding, which the mock has at 4px but the selected ring uses 2px+2px offset so it clips against the 4px padding by about 0).

### Selection and state not relying on color
- **[high]** Stale preview is faded with no visible text (EXPERIENCE.md Voice and Tone, State Patterns). Assessment: (1) Sighted users who see the dim have a cue, but it is a pure luminance/opacity difference (1.4.1 concerns color; opacity is not color, so technically allowed). (2) Low vision and high-contrast/forced-colors users: opacity may be ignored or the difference is imperceptible with brand gradients (above). (3) Screen reader users: the live-region sentence is transient; a user who moves away and returns to the preview finds an `img` named "Generated code" with no state, because the state is not in the DOM persistently. (4) Cognitive users: the only persistent visible signal is Generate enabled vs disabled, which is itself only an opacity difference. Download-off is also opacity. The decision "no text on or over the QR" is fine; the decision "no visible text anywhere" is not. *Fix:* keep the QR clean, but add a persistent status line OUTSIDE the QR frame (small `hint` text under the frame, "Up to date" / "Settings changed: press Generate"), and set the preview image's accessible description to include state (`aria-describedby` pointing at that line). This satisfies sighted, SR, low vision and cognitive needs without covering the code. The user may still reject visible text; if so, minimum compensations are: persistent `aria-describedby` state text, a non-opacity cue on the frame (dashed border vs solid, which also survives forced-colors), and the Generate/Download disabled reasons.
- **[medium]** Disabled Generate when fresh and disabled Download when stale are distinguished only by opacity and `cursor`. If implemented with the `disabled` attribute the buttons leave the tab order, and the stated "accessible description explaining the reason" (Accessibility Floor) is unreachable for keyboard and SR users because disabled buttons are not focusable (4.1.2, 3.3.2). *Fix:* use `aria-disabled="true"` with the button remaining focusable, `aria-describedby` pointing at the reason, and block activation in JS; or keep `disabled` and put the reason in visible adjacent text. Specify which in EXPERIENCE.md. Note a focus-loss trap too: when Generate is pressed it becomes disabled and, if natively disabled, focus drops to body.
- **[medium]** Solid/Gradient toggle active state is accent fill + white text; the inactive state is muted text on surface-2. The shape difference (fill vs no fill) is acceptable but only 4.32:1 text; and the segmented toggle is called a radio group while visually a button pair. Fine if `role=radiogroup` with `aria-checked`, but spec should say the group has an accessible name ("Color mode") and the radios have names.
- **[medium]** Direction buttons: icons (arrows and a circle glyph) with `aria-label`. The mock uses unicode glyphs (→ ↓ ↘ ↗ ◎) as visual labels, which are not accessible-name sources but fine as decoration. Direction semantics are unclear for the radial option (first color = center); the 'Radial' label should say "Radial, first color at center". Also: arrow glyph direction (diagonal "down-right") may not match what the gradient does for the second color; users with cognitive disabilities need a live mini-preview, which the preset thumbnails redrawing partly gives.
- **[medium]** Scan warning: danger color + icon + text is fine for 1.4.1. The Download "warning icon" must have a text alternative (`aria-label`/visually hidden "may not scan") or the button name stays "Download PNG" and SR users lose the cue; in the mock the glyph is inside the button text ("⚠ ⇩ Download PNG"), which a screen reader reads as "warning sign down arrow Download PNG" unless hidden. *Fix:* hide the glyphs with `aria-hidden` and add the text to the button's description.
- **[low]** Saved-logo selection ring is the same as the preset ring; fine, but saved-logo tiles are 56px *letters/images with no visible labels* (mock uses "M", "A", "K"). Need an `aria-label` with a user-provided name (logos have filenames; spec does not say names are stored or shown). Specify.

### Keyboard and focus
- **[high]** Radio group keyboard behavior is underspecified and partly wrong. EXPERIENCE.md Interaction Primitives says "arrow keys move the selection, Space or Enter confirms" which describes a *focus-without-select* model, whereas the ARIA radio group pattern is: arrow keys move focus AND select, Space selects the focused if not selected, Tab enters the group at the checked item (roving tabindex), Tab leaves it. Selecting a preset marks the code stale and redraws; if arrows select-on-move, the stale/announce churn is high. Decide and state: (a) native radio semantics (arrows select), or (b) a listbox/toolbar-like pattern with manual activation. Also specify: roving `tabindex` (checked item 0, others -1), Home/End, wrap, and that the horizontal strip scrolls the focused item into view (`scrollIntoView({inline:'nearest'})`). The mock puts only the selected thumbnail as `role=radio` but all strip items need the same; they also do not wrap in a `role=radiogroup` with an accessible name ("Style preset") in the default mock (only direction has `aria-label` on the group).
- **[high]** Focus management after Fix is unspecified. The Fix button is inside the scan-warning banner and applying it changes a control elsewhere (logo size slider) and then marks the code stale; the banner may remain ("stays until regenerated code decodes") but the Fix label now describes a change already made. Focus is on a button that now has stale meaning. Specify: after Fix, move focus to the changed control (so the 'changed to 15%' announcement coincides with focus, and the glow cue lands where the user is), or keep focus on Fix but update its label/disable it with "applied". Also say what happens when the Fix target is in the form column and the user is on a narrow screen (focus move plus scroll).
- **[high]** Floating Generate (<=900px) overlaps content and can obscure the focused element (2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured, minimum). A sticky 12px-from-bottom full-width pill 44-50px tall covers the bottom ~65px of the viewport; tabbing down the form leaves the focused field under it. Reflow zoom makes it worse (at 400% zoom, a 320 CSS px layout, a full-width pill takes 1/5 to 1/3 of the viewport height). *Fix:* set `scroll-padding-bottom` on the document to the pill height plus margin so focused elements scroll clear of it; do not float at all at zoom >= 200% or short viewports (`@media (max-height: 500px)`), fall back to inline; make sure the pill is the last in tab order *and* a reasonable early shortcut is not required (it is DOM-wise after the form, but visually floating; it must not trap the reading order).
- **[medium]** Auto-scroll to the preview on Generate (narrow): focus remains on the floating button (or in a content field if triggered by Enter) while the viewport jumps; sighted keyboard users lose their place, SR users get no cue that the preview is now in view. Specify: after generation, announce via the live region and move focus to the preview region heading or the status text (a programmatic `tabindex=-1` target), or avoid the scroll when the preview is already partly visible. Also respect `prefers-reduced-motion` (use instant scroll, not smooth).
- **[medium]** Focus order versus visual order: DOM order must be Content, Code Settings, Colors, Logo, Style, then preview, banner, Generate, Download (matches spec). But on narrow screens the floating Generate is visually at the bottom while its DOM position is after Style; fine. On wide screens, the right panel is sticky so a keyboard user who has tabbed through the form must go through the whole form (including a 7-item preset strip, 5 direction buttons, saved-logo strip up to 20 tiles, each with a delete button) to reach Generate. Rovings reduce stops, but the 20 saved-logo tiles each with its own delete icon are 20+ tab stops if not a roving group. *Fix:* make the saved-logo strip a radiogroup (roving) with the delete control reachable via a documented key (Delete key, or a single "Delete selected logo" button after the strip) rather than 20 icon buttons; also provide a "Skip to preview and Generate" link or landmarks (`<main>`, `<form>`, `<aside aria-label="Preview">`).
- **[medium]** Delete in-place confirm "Delete? Yes / No": the tile is replaced in place, so focus is destroyed with the removed element. Specify: focus moves to "No" (safe default) on open; Esc cancels and returns focus to the delete icon; after Yes, focus goes to the next tile (or the drop zone if empty), and the result ("Logo deleted") is announced. The confirm text should be announced (it should be a `role=group` with label or an `alert`).
- **[medium]** Scrollable strips: EXPERIENCE.md says "keyboard-scrollable and remain focusable". The roving radios handle preset keyboarding, but for the logo strip and any non-interactive overflow, a scroll container with no focusable children is not keyboard-scrollable in some browsers; Chrome now focuses scrollers, Safari/Firefox historically do not (2.1.1). With roving radio groups, scrolling is by focus; with a container `tabindex=0` you create an extra stop. Specify: strip = radiogroup with roving tabindex and `scrollIntoView`; do NOT add `tabindex=0` on the scroller when it has focusable children (axe rule `scrollable-region-focusable` is satisfied by focusable children).
- **[low]** Enter in a content field triggers Generate "only while enabled": the failure is silent. A keyboard user pressing Enter when Generate is disabled (fresh code) gets nothing, which is fine, but specify no action and no announcement, or "Already up to date" through the live region.

### Screen reader
- **[high]** Live-region strategy is under-specified and risks both noise and misses. "Stale, fresh, generating and scan-warning states are announced through an aria-live region (polite)" plus "scan warning and contrast warning announced when they appear" plus "Logo size changed to 15%" plus "Download is available" plus "Download unavailable: preview is out of date": every preset arrow-key press marks the code stale, so each press would announce "Download unavailable...". Specify: announce stale only on the transition fresh to stale (once per generate cycle, not per change); do not announce "Download is available" separately from "Generated"; merge into one message ("Generated. Download available. Scan warning: ...") to avoid queue floods; use one persistent, pre-existing live region (`role=status`) rather than injecting new ones (a region created at the same time as its content is often not announced, and the mock's `role=alert` on the warning div is injected content).
- **[medium]** Scan warning as `role=alert` (assertive) as in the mock: it interrupts, and arrives right after the "Generated" status, causing one to clobber the other. It is a result of a user-triggered action and actionable, so assertive is defensible, but pick one: either `role=alert` for the warning and `role=status` for everything else, with the warning message delivered once and the Fix button reachable next; or make both polite and sequence them in one message. Recommend: single polite status "Generated. This code may not scan: the logo covers too much. A fix is available." and leave the banner `role=group` aria-label "Scan warning" with no live semantics (avoid double announcement of status + alert).
- **[medium]** Contrast warning: spine says "announced when appears" but typing a hex field mid-edit toggles it on every keystroke (live validation against partial hex). Specify announce on blur/commit or debounce, and `aria-describedby` from the offending color input to the warning, plus `aria-invalid`.
- **[medium]** Preview image alt: baseline `alt="Generated code"` is not informative or stateful. The QR content is the data; the alt should say what it encodes in summary form ("QR code for https://example.com", truncated for long content), and the state should be separate (see stale finding). Placeholder state ("Configure and generate") must not be an image with empty alt that a user must find.
- **[medium]** Names/roles/states for thumbnails: `aria-label="Square"` with a tooltip `title`. The accessible name is fine; but `title` also feeds the accessible description, so the SR reads "Square, Square". Also: names alone ("Fluid", "Circle") do not convey what the shape is; spec says sighted users read the shape from the thumbnail, so SR users get only a name; add a one-clause description for each preset in the preset table (`aria-describedby` or the label "Fluid, rounded flowing dots"). Remove `title` duplication or make the tooltip a custom one with `aria-describedby` and dismissable on Esc (1.4.13: dismissible, hoverable, persistent).
- **[medium]** Announcement when Fix changes a control: spec has "Logo size changed to 15%" but it competes with the focus move. If focus moves to the changed control (recommended), the control's own name and new value are announced automatically and the live message should be short or omitted.
- **[low]** The Solid/Gradient and direction groups need group names; color fields need visible-label association; the swatch `input type=color` is a native control with poor SR output ("color well"); the hex field is the accessible path, so label it ("Foreground color, hex") and say the swatch is supplementary.
- **[low]** Barcode tab switching hides Colors, Logo and Style: specify `hidden`/`display:none` (removes from the a11y tree) not just `visibility` tricks, and announce nothing or "Colors, Logo and Style are available for QR codes only" in a hint so users do not lose options silently ("silent state changes" are banned in spec). Format tabs in baseline are plain buttons (no `role=tablist`, no `aria-pressed`/`aria-selected`) so current tab state is color-only (accent fill): this is inherited, but the new spec adds hidden/shown sections to it. *Fix:* use `aria-pressed` or tab semantics.

### Motor and touch
- **[high]** Delete icon on saved-logo corner: 18px circle in the mock, offset -6px outside the 56px tile (WCAG 2.5.8 requires 24x24 CSS px or sufficient spacing; the circle is 18px and its spacing circles with the tile and adjacent tiles within a 12px gap). It also overlaps the tile, which is the selectable target, so a mis-tap selects a logo instead of deleting, or the reverse. *Fix:* at least 24px target (44px on touch), with padding rather than glyph size, positioned inside the tile or as a separate row action; or, per the roving-group fix, a single "Delete" button under the strip for the selected logo.
- **[medium]** Direction buttons are 36px: meets 2.5.8 (24px) but under the 44px best practice; the gap is 6px. Acceptable for AA; recommend 44px on `pointer: coarse`. Preset thumbnails at 72px and logo tiles at 56px pass.
- **[medium]** Horizontal scrolling strips are a pointer-only trap for some users: scrolling requires a horizontal swipe or a trackpad; mouse-only users without a horizontal wheel must use the scrollbar (kept visible, good), but 8px scrollbar thickness is a very small target. 7 presets at 72px + 12px gaps are about 580px: wider than the 372px content width at 420px form (3.5 items visible), so 3+ presets are off-screen and a user does not know there are more (no visible count, no fade hint by decision). *Fix:* either wrap presets in a grid (7 items need two rows; no scrolling needed, and removes 1.4.10 two-dimensional scrolling concerns), or add visible prev/next buttons plus "1 of 7" position text. A grid is the stronger accessible option; the "horizontal strip" decision was made for compactness, so call this out as a trade-off to revisit.
- **[medium]** Drag-and-drop for logo upload: the drop zone should be a real button / label for a file input (keyboard and click operable; spec says "click or drag" so it likely is, but state that the drop zone is a focusable control with an accessible name and that drag is never required, 2.5.7 Dragging Movements).
- **[low]** Sliders (logo size, margin): native range inputs are fine; a 16px thumb is under 24px (2.5.8) only when no alternative: native keyboard arrows exist, which counts as an alternative. Show the numeric value (done).
- **[low]** Floating Generate full-width pill could cover the bottom of the scan warning and the Download buttons on the narrow layout, which sit at the end of the page (spacer needed so the page bottom is reachable: add `padding-bottom` equal to the pill height).

### Reflow, zoom, resize
- **[high]** The 420px fixed form column and 280px fixed preview frame: at <=900px the grid is one column, so the 420px is gone; but between 901 and ~1000px the two-column layout leaves the preview about 480px, and at 200% to 400% zoom the effective viewport shrinks under 900px so the one-column layout kicks in: good. The risk is the *preview card* in the narrow mock (`.pframe` fixed 280px, `.card` padding 1.25rem, 390px frame) and the 5 direction buttons (5x36 + 4x6 = 204px, fine), the 4-up EC grid, and the `.row2` two-column grids for Size/Margin that do not stack at 320px. Verify 1.4.10 at 320 CSS px: no horizontal page scroll except the strips (strips are data-ish content so allowed, but the preview frame fixed at 280px plus 2x2rem card padding and form 1.5rem padding is 280+40+... fits 320 only barely, and hex fields in `.row2` at 1fr 1fr of 272px will truncate). Specify the min-width target as 320px and `max-width:100%` on frame; the sticky preview must be `position: static` below 900px and also when height < ~600px (sticky preview taller than the viewport at 200% to 400% zoom becomes unreachable, since its bottom Download buttons scroll out with no scroll of their own).
- **[medium]** Sticky preview below the 60px header on wide screens: at 200% zoom on a 1280px display (640 CSS px) layout is already one column, fine; but at 150% to 175% zoom (about 730 to 850 CSS px) it stays two-column with the card (up to 460px + chrome) taller than the viewport, so Generate/Download can be cut off. Add `max-height: calc(100vh - 60px); overflow:auto` or release stickiness for short viewports. Also a sticky element can obscure focus targets in the form column from above (2.4.11): the header is sticky-ish at 60px; add `scroll-margin-top`/`scroll-padding-top: 76px`.
- **[medium]** Text spacing (1.4.12): labels at 0.65rem to 0.78rem with fixed-height controls (36px buttons, 72px tiles, 22px toggle) must not clip when users override line height/letter spacing; the logo tile letters and 0.6rem delete glyph are especially fragile. Use min-height, not height, for text-bearing controls. Hint size 0.72rem and section title 0.65rem (about 10.4px) are very small; zoom handles, but 0.65rem uppercase bold with letter-spacing is on the edge of legibility; consider 0.75rem minimum.
- **[low]** Orientation/landscape phones: floating pill + sticky header can take up to 40% of a 320px-tall landscape viewport. See the `max-height` fallback above.

### Motion and transients
- **[high]** The 2-second "Generated" banner (State Patterns; baseline `setTimeout 2000`) is a time-limited message: 2.2.1 applies to *time limits on content the user needs to read*; a 2s toast for a sighted user is too short for many users (slow readers, magnification users who see only a part of the screen) and is not guaranteed to be announced to SR users depending on how it is injected (4.1.3 requires a status message to be programmatically determinable without focus; it needs to be in a `role=status` region present beforehand). It also fights with the scan warning for the same slot. *Fix:* put "Generated" into the persistent status line suggested above (replaced, not removed, on the next state change); if a transient is kept, make it at least 5 s (preferably until the next user action) and ensure the SR announcement does not depend on it.
- **[medium]** 1.5 second accent glow on the Fix-changed control: a transient visual cue at 15% accent alpha is nearly invisible (below 1.1:1 against the surface) and the spec admits it must not be the only signal. It needs a persistent equivalent, e.g. a visible text note next to the changed control ("Changed from 30% to 22% by Fix", dismiss on next edit), which also serves cognitive users who lose track of what Fix did. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for the glow transition and the spinner (static "Generating..." text instead of or with the spinner), and the smooth auto-scroll.
- **[low]** Spinner overlaying the dimmed code needs a text equivalent (`aria-busy` on the preview region and the status text "Generating"); a decorative spinner is silent. Also announce generation done even if it takes under a second (debounce "Generating" announcements for fast results to avoid double messages).

### Missing from the spines (needed to build correctly)
- **[high]** No landmarks or heading structure: define `<header>`, `<main>`, form sections as `<fieldset><legend>` or `<section aria-labelledby>` with real headings for the 5 section titles (they are styled 0.65rem labels in the mock, i.e. `<p>`); without headings SR users cannot navigate Content/Colors/Logo/Style. Preview region as `<section aria-labelledby>` or `role=region` with name.
- **[high]** No focus-visible spec (see contrast section) and no forced-colors/high-contrast spec: opacity, border-color-only and box-shadow-glow states vanish in Windows High Contrast; the selection ring uses `outline` (good, it survives forced-colors), the glow (`box-shadow`) does not. Specify `outline` for all focus/selection cues and `forced-colors` fallbacks (e.g. `border: 2px solid Highlight` for selected, `ButtonText` for borders, and the stale frame uses a dashed border).
- **[medium]** No error-handling accessibility spec: logo rejection and generation errors need `role=alert` or association via `aria-describedby`/`aria-errormessage` on the drop zone; required-field error "names it" so the field needs `aria-invalid` and focus must move to the first invalid field (3.3.1, 3.3.3).
- **[medium]** Theme toggle (36px circle in the mock, no label) needs an accessible name and state (`aria-pressed` or "Switch to light theme"), and the setting should respect `prefers-color-scheme` initially (spec says dark default; fine, but mention).
- **[medium]** The "tooltip" is spec'd as supplementary but unspecified: if custom, it needs 1.4.13 behavior (hoverable, dismissible with Esc, persistent) and keyboard-focus display; if native `title`, it is not keyboard- or touch-reachable and duplicates the label. Pick one; recommend dropping tooltips for presets and showing the selected preset's name as visible text *below the strip* ("Selected: Fluid"), which also addresses low vision, cognitive load and touch users with one cheap line (this reverses the 'no visible labels' decision only for the selected item; note it as a trade-off).
- **[medium]** Language/readability: microcopy is good. Add `lang="en"` (mocks have it) and reading-level sanity ("EC", "Margin" jargon in labels: "Error correction" is spelled out in the spine, keep it spelled out in the UI with the hint).
- **[low]** Authentication/redundant-entry rules (3.3.7/3.3.8) are not relevant; Consistent Help (3.2.6) N/A.
